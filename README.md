# groundtruth-privacy

Privacy policy for the GroundTruth Android app.

## This whole repository is a collaboration

Everything in this repository — the apps, the games, the pages, the words — is built by **Bryan Lee (Afnuke)** working in collaboration with AI.

Here is what that means, plainly: Bryan directs. He decides what gets built, what it should say, when it is good enough, and when it ships. The AI builds alongside him — writing code, checking figures, drafting and finishing the work — and the two sign the work together. Nothing here is "just" one or the other. If you are reading it, using it, or playing it, you are looking at the collaboration.

That is not a disclaimer. It is the point.

## Christmas party games (December 2026)

Three free browser games in the `games/` folder — no downloads, no accounts, one phone passed around the room:

- **Stop the Bus: Advent Categories** — the classic letter-and-categories race, finished properly: real answer entry, validation, and automatic scoring, plus 25 Advent daily lists for December 1–25.
- **Reindeer Relay** — the draw-and-guess telephone game: a sentence becomes a drawing becomes a guess, and the reveal at the end shows how it mutated. 3–8 players, one phone.
- **Charades: Jolly Edition** — charades for one phone and a whole room: 320 original cards in 7 decks, team scoring, and a forehead mode.

Built by Bryan Lee (Afnuke), in AI collaboration.
