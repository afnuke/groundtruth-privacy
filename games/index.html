🤝 About the Collaboration

Let's say it plain, because it's true and we're proud of it: everything here — the games, the apps, the words on this page — is built by Bryan Lee (Afnuke) working in collaboration with AI. Not “made by a company.” Not churned out by a machine on its own. A man and an AI, building together on purpose.

🧑 Bryan calls the shots

Every idea starts with him. He picks the games, sets the style, makes the calls, and says what's good enough to carry his name — and what goes back in the shop. If it's here, it's because he said so.

🤖 The AI builds alongside him

The AI does the heavy lifting at the workbench — writing the code, laying out the pages, checking the math — and brings the work back to Bryan for the verdict. A partner at the bench, not the boss of it.

That's the deal on every project in this repository: he directs, decides, and calls the shots; the AI builds alongside him; and the pair sign their work together. Two names on the door, because both of them did the work.

Bryan Lee (Afnuke) × AI — signed together, like everything else here.

📌 A Note from the Workshop

Straight talk, the way we'd want it told to us: these three games were polished with care and checked end to end before they went out — but they have not been through a long beta with a big crowd of testers. They were publishable, so we published them. If something breaks, sticks, or just feels off when you play, we'd rather hear about it than hide it — that's how the next version gets better.
