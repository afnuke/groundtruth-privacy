# Three Jolly Games — Christmas party games

Three free browser games. No downloads, no accounts, no servers — each one is a single page you open on a phone and pass around the room.

## The games

### Stop the Bus: Advent Categories
The traditional Categories game ("Stop the Bus"), finished the way it always should have been: a letter and a list of categories, a 2-minute clock, real answer entry, validation, and automatic scoring. Solo mode grades your answers against what everybody typically writes — crowd favorites score low, rare finds score high. Party mode passes the phone between 2–4 players and cancels matching answers automatically. Includes 25 Advent daily lists, one for each day of December 1–25, and friend challenges by code.

### Reindeer Relay
The folk draw-and-guess telephone game, under an original name. One player starts with a secret sentence; the next draws it; the next guesses the drawing; the phone travels the room until the final reveal shows the whole chain — and how far it wandered. 3–8 players, one phone, with a full drawing pad (10 colors, eraser, brush sizes, undo), Christmas and everyday prompt decks, and playful "mutation awards" at the reveal.

### Charades: Jolly Edition
The classic parlour game for one phone and a whole room. 320 original cards across 7 decks (Christmas Chaos, Movie Magic, Animal Antics, Actions & Jobs, Famous Places, Easy/Kids, and a 25-card Advent deck), team scoring, 30/60/90-second rounds, and an optional forehead mode.

All three games carry gentle background music: a music-box loop of public-domain Christmas carols (Jingle Bells, Deck the Halls, We Wish You a Merry Christmas), synthesized in the page — no audio files.
