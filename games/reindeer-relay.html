Reindeer Relay

One phone. One sentence. A room full of artists who cannot draw. Pass it, draw it, guess it — and watch a perfectly good sentence turn into beautiful nonsense.

How to play (30 seconds)
  1. Player 1 gets a secret sentence. Read it, memorize it, guard it with your life.
  2. Pass the phone. The next player sees only the sentence and has to draw it. No words in the drawing — that's cheating, Santa is watching.
  3. Pass again. The next player sees only the drawing and writes what they think it is.
  4. Draw, guess, draw, guess… all the way around the room.
  5. At the end, the phone reveals the whole chain — and you watch "Santa stuck in a chimney" become "a sausage escaping a house fire." That's the game. That's the whole game. It's glorious.
