How to play — the 20-second version
  1. Team Holly goes first. One Holly player grabs the phone — they're the actor. No talking, no mouthing words, no pointing at things. Pure mime chaos.
  2. The card shows a word or phrase. The actor acts it out while their team shouts guesses like their lives depend on it.
  3. Guessed it? Smash CORRECT. Hopeless? Hit PASS and move on — no shame, some cards are just evil.
  4. When the timer buzzes, the round's over. Count the haul, pass the phone to Team Jingle, and repeat.
  5. After every team has played all their rounds, the high score takes the crown — and bragging rights until New Year's.
