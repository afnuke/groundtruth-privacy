Walkthrough Training

You film the walk. We build the training.

Some jobs aren't learned from a manual. They're learned by walking them — the round, the route, the checks, in the right order. Walkthrough Training turns a video of your walk into an interactive tutorial, so a new hire can run it once and know the job before their first shift.

How it works

1

You film your walkthrough

Walk the job the way it's actually done and record it — a phone camera is fine. The real layout, the real checkpoints, the real order of work.

2

We build the tutorial from it

Your footage becomes a hands-on training run: your layout, your checkpoints, your order of work, with the scanner or phone as the narrator, talking the new hire through each step.

3

Your new hires run it once

Before their first shift, they walk the whole job on a phone — every stop, every check, in order. When they step on site, it's not their first time through.

It stays in house

The tutorial is built for your people only. It stays in house, under your control — it doesn't go on our site, and it doesn't go anywhere public. Your footage is reference only, and nothing about your operation goes public. The only public part is this page, offering the service.

Who it's for

Any job that's learned by walking it:

  • Security rounds and post checks
  • Warehouse walks and inventory routes
  • Facility inspections and building checks
  • Delivery and service routes

If a new person has to learn it on foot, it can be taught this way.

Where it started

The first walkthrough training build came from a real working security round — filmed on the job, checkpoint by checkpoint, first hour to last. When it was done, the company asked to see it. That's the whole idea: training built from the actual work, not a stock video of somebody else's building.

What it costs

First builds are being taken on now. There's no price list yet — every walk is different. Write and describe your walk, and we'll talk it through.
